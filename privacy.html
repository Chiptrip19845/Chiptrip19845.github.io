Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-04

Application: Steamy Personal Assistant (a private, single-user application). Developer and only user: the owner of the connected Google account.

1. Data the application accesses

  • Google Calendar (read-only): event titles, times and locations of the owner's own calendars.
  • Gmail: messages and drafts in the owner's own mailbox, to summarise them and to prepare drafts. Messages are sent only after the owner's explicit approval.

2. How the data is used

Data is used only to provide the assistant's features to the owner: a daily overview and help writing email. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

3. Storage and sharing

  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in files readable by the owner only.
  • Email and calendar content is processed on the owner's computer and by the AI model service the owner has configured, solely to answer the owner's requests. It is not sold, shared with third parties for their own use, or published.
  • No data is collected from any other person.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Retention and deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the locally stored tokens. After revocation the application can no longer access any Google data.

6. Contact

steamy@agentmail.to
