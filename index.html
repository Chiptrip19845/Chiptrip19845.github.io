Steamy Personal Assistant

A private, single-user assistant that runs on its owner's own computer. It connects to the owner's own Google account to read calendar entries for a daily overview and to read, draft and, only on the owner's explicit instruction, send email.

What it does

  • Reads calendar events (read-only) to prepare a daily briefing.
  • Reads email and prepares drafts for the owner to review. Messages are sent only after the owner approves them.

Who uses it

This application is not offered to the public. It is used only by its developer, for his own Google account.
